FAQ

Why no operator?

Because an operator is a single point of failure, a custodian, and a capture target. Commons Pool eliminates all three by design.

How do I connect?

Point a Stratum V2 miner at signet.commonspool.org:3334. Username is your Bitcoin address. Password can be x. Job declaration on. Signet only. Not live on mainnet.

How do I get paid?

Directly from the coinbase output of every block the pool finds. Your address, your funds, no withdrawal step. PPLNS over the last finalized snapshot. Fee is 1%.

Do I need to KYC?

No. Your identity is a secp256k1 keypair.

Who ships Commons Pool?

BTCDecoded. Commons Pool is a BTCDecoded project. Spec, node, and governance live under that org. btcdecoded.org

What is Bitcoin Commons?

The underlying node implementation Commons Pool runs on. thebitcoincommons.org

What is signet?

Bitcoin's test network. The status page is test data. Not a live pool. Not mainnet.

When mainnet?

When it works. No date.
